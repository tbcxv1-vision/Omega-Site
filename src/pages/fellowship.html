Fellowship questions

Do I need prior tech experience?{{icon:ChevronDown}}

No. The program starts with foundations in month one — we meet you where you are. Curiosity and commitment matter more than experience.

Who is eligible to apply?{{icon:ChevronDown}}

High school students aged 16 and older, college students pursuing IT, computer science or related fields, and recent graduates looking for real-world experience.

How long is the program and how big is the cohort?{{icon:ChevronDown}}

The fellowship runs six months. Our first cohort is capped at 10 fellows so everyone gets real mentorship.

Will I work on real projects?{{icon:ChevronDown}}

Yes. Month four is dedicated to hands-on team projects, and in month five fellows shadow and assist on real client engagements under direct mentor supervision.

When are applications reviewed?{{icon:ChevronDown}}

Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis, so applying early improves your chance of a seat in Cohort 1.

I work in tech — how can I help?{{icon:ChevronDown}}

We're looking for industry volunteers to teach, mentor, and guide fellows. Tell us about your expertise and we'll be in touch.
