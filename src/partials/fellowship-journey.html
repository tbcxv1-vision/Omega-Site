The six-month journey

A structured path from foundations to career launch.

  1. {{icon:GraduationCap}}Month 1

    Foundations & Onboarding

    STEM fundamentals, IT basics, and an introduction to cybersecurity principles. Cohort bonding and goal-setting.

  2. {{icon:Cpu}}Month 2

    Core Technical Skills

    Networking, systems, and operating systems in depth. Daily labs build real hands-on capability.

  3. {{icon:Terminal}}Month 3

    Cybersecurity Concepts

    Threats, defense, cryptography, and secure architecture. Capture-the-flag challenges reinforce theory.

  4. {{icon:Briefcase}}Month 4

    Hands-On Projects

    Build real security tools and infrastructure in team labs, reviewed by senior engineers.

  5. {{icon:Network}}Month 5

    Supervised Client Work

    Shadow and assist on real client engagements under direct mentor supervision. Apply what you learned.

  6. {{icon:Rocket}}Month 6

    Capstone & Career Launch

    Deliver a capstone project, refine your resume, and prep for certifications and interviews.

  • {{icon:Trophy}}

  • {{icon:Users}}
