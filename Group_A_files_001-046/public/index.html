// Fellowship program

The Omega STEM Fellowship

A six-month immersive program that takes high school students, college students, and recent graduates from curiosity to career — through teaching, mentoring, hands-on projects, and supervised real-world work with our clients.

  • High School Students

  • College Students

  • Recent Graduates

    Bridge the gap between graduation and a role with mentorship and experience.

The six-month journey

A structured path from foundations to career launch.

  1. Month 1

    Foundations & Onboarding

    STEM fundamentals, IT basics, and an introduction to cybersecurity principles. Cohort bonding and goal-setting.

  2. Month 2

    Core Technical Skills

    Networking, systems, and operating systems in depth. Daily labs build real hands-on capability.

  3. Month 3

    Cybersecurity Concepts

    Threats, defense, cryptography, and secure architecture. Capture-the-flag challenges reinforce theory.

  4. Month 4

    Hands-On Projects

    Build real security tools and infrastructure in team labs, reviewed by senior engineers.

  5. Month 5

    Supervised Client Work

    Shadow and assist on real client engagements under direct mentor supervision. Apply what you learned.

  6. Month 6

    Capstone & Career Launch

    Deliver a capstone project, refine your resume, and prep for certifications and interviews.

What fellows get

  • Real-World Experience

    Supervised work on live client engagements — not just classroom theory.

  • 1:1 Mentorship

    Dedicated mentors guide each fellow through technical and professional growth.

  • STEM + IT + Cyber

    A blended curriculum spanning STEM foundations to advanced security topics.

  • Career Development

    Resume building, certification prep, and interview coaching to launch careers.

Who can apply

  • High school students (16+) with an interest in STEM or tech
  • College students pursuing IT, CS, or related fields
  • Recent graduates looking for real-world experience
  • Curious, motivated learners ready to commit 6 months
  • No prior experience required — we meet you where you are

Cohort 1: capped at 10 fellows. Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis.
